Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To provide a stimulating learning environment with a technological orientation, maximizing individual potential and ensuring the uplifting of students of all ability levels, well-equipped to meet the challenges of education, work, and life.

Mission

To develop in every student an active and creative mind, a sense of understanding and compassion for others, and the courage to act on their beliefs. The total development includes spiritual, moral, intellectual, social, emotional, and physical growth.

Extracurricular Activities

Sports and More

DPS Bareilly offers a wide range of sports activities with world-class infrastructure and renowned coaches. Our dedicated sports academy provides programs in Cricket, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Volleyball, Swimming, and many more.

Soft Skills Development

We focus on the holistic development of our students, teaching them interpersonal skills, social etiquette, communication, and leadership. We believe in nurturing creativity and innovation through various clubs and activities like the Dramatics Club and the Puerile Writers Club.
